gpxcam time zone

Set the time zone that a SenseCAP T1000-E card with gpxcam gives your LUMIX camera.

This browser cannot talk to the card.

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux). Firefox, Safari and phones do not support Web Serial.

You can also set the time zone with a serial terminal, see the README.

1. Connect the card

Plug the card into this computer with a USB cable, click Connect and choose T1000-E gpxcam. Close other programs that use the card first (a serial monitor).

    3. Save it on the card

    The camera gets the new time zone the next time the card connects to it: switch the camera off and on. The card connects only when its GPS has a fix.

    About this page

    The page runs only in your browser and sends nothing over the internet. It talks to the card over USB at 115200 baud and sends it only these commands: v (version), tz (show the time zone) and tz with the zone you chose. Offsets shown in the list come from your browser and are only a guide; the card uses its own copy of the tz database.
