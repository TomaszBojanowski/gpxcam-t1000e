One more step for this camera

This is an older camera (LUMIX Sync generation). It needs the card registered once over its own Wi-Fi.

On this computer, join the Wi-Fi network the camera shows on its screen.

No password, unless you set one in the camera.

This step needs the gpxcam card app: a browser cannot reach the camera's Wi-Fi. Cancel, then pair it in the app.

Paired with your camera

From now on the card connects by itself whenever the camera is on. You can unplug it.

Forget the paired camera? The card stops connecting to it. To pair them again later, also delete T1000‑E in the camera's Bluetooth menu.

Your camera stays paired as before.

  • Start pairing on the camera only when you are told to – some cameras stay in pairing mode for just 30 seconds.
  • Keep the camera within a metre of the card.
  • Check that no phone is connected to the camera.

An older camera? (S5, S1, GH5, GH6, G9, G100…)

Pair recognises most of them by itself. If it did not, pair yours as an older camera: the same steps, plus one over the camera's Wi-Fi.

The step over the camera's Wi-Fi needs the gpxcam card app.

How pairing works
  • Pairing needs the time from the card's GPS: put the card where it gets a fix, outdoors or by a window.
  • Older cameras (LUMIX Sync generation) need one more step, over the camera's own Wi-Fi network, which only the gpxcam card app can do.

    Diagnostics

    When something does not work, this collects what the card knows, ready to paste into a message. Copy log takes the card's last events. Scan for cameras listens for 30 seconds and lists the Bluetooth devices next to the card: put the camera next to it first, with Bluetooth pairing started on the camera.

    What is copied hides your camera's serial number, the Bluetooth addresses and the card's identity.

    About this page

    The page runs only in your browser and sends nothing over the internet. It talks to the card over USB at 115200 baud and sends it only v, status, pair, pair off, pair older, pair prepare, p, unpair, clock on, clock off, updates normal, updates frequent, sound loud, sound quiet, sound off, log, diag scan, tz and tz with the zone you chose. Offsets in the list come from your browser and are only a guide; the card uses its own copy of the tz database.
