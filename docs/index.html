Your SenseCAP T1000‑E with gpxcam: its state, pairing with your LUMIX camera, and its settings.

This browser cannot talk to the card. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux): Firefox, Safari and phones do not support Web Serial. On a computer without Chrome or Edge, use the desktop app, gpxcam card. A serial terminal works too, see the README.

The camera

Connect the card to pair it with your camera.

Pairing from this page needs gpxcam 1.3.0. With gpxcam 1.2, pair with the card's button, see the README.

How to pair
  1. Put the card where its GPS gets a fix, outdoors or by a window: pairing needs the time from it. The GPS tile above shows Fix when it is ready.
  2. If the camera lists T1000‑E from an earlier pairing, delete it there first: the camera does not pair a card it already knows.
  3. Click Pair. Do not start pairing on the camera yet: some cameras stay in pairing mode only about 30 seconds, so the card must already be looking for them.
  4. When the page says Now start Bluetooth pairing on the camera, start pairing in the camera's Bluetooth menu. The card looks for it for up to 2 minutes and tries up to 3 times.

Forget the paired camera? The card stops connecting to it. To pair them again later, also delete T1000‑E in the camera's Bluetooth menu.

Pair an older camera

Older LUMIX cameras (the ones that pair with the older Panasonic phone app, not LUMIX Lab) need one extra step, over the camera's own Wi-Fi network, which the app does for you. Use this only for such a camera – for an S5II and the like, use Pair above.

How it works
  1. Put the card where its GPS gets a fix, outdoors or by a window. The app switches the GPS on when it connects; the GPS tile above shows Fix when it is ready.
  2. Click Start. Do not start pairing on the camera yet: it stays in pairing mode only about 30 seconds, so the card must already be looking for it.
  3. When the app says Now start Bluetooth pairing on the camera, do it (the camera's Bluetooth menu → Pairing). The card introduces itself; the camera then shows a Wi-Fi network and a password.
  4. On this computer, join that Wi-Fi network by hand. The app finishes the pairing and tells you how it went.

Before pairing, remove the phone from the camera's Bluetooth device list (Setup → Bluetooth → Delete), or at least turn the phone's Bluetooth off. A phone that is still registered may grab the camera the moment it starts advertising, and the card will not get in.

    Diagnostics

    When something does not work, this collects what the card knows, ready to paste into a message. Copy log takes the card's last events. Scan for cameras listens for 30 seconds and lists the Bluetooth devices next to the card: put the camera next to it first, with Bluetooth pairing started on the camera.

    What is copied hides your camera's serial number, the Bluetooth addresses and the card's identity.

    About this page

    The page runs only in your browser and sends nothing over the internet. It talks to the card over USB at 115200 baud and sends it only v, status, pair, pair off, pair older, pair prepare, unpair, clock on, clock off, updates normal, updates frequent, sound loud, sound quiet, sound off, log, diag scan, tz and tz with the zone you chose. Offsets in the list come from your browser and are only a guide; the card uses its own copy of the tz database.
