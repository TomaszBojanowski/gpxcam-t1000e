The camera

Connect the card to pair it with your camera.

Pairing from this page needs gpxcam 1.3.0. With gpxcam 1.2, pair with the card's button, see the README.

  1. Put the card where its GPS gets a fix, outdoors or by a window: pairing needs the time from it.
  2. On the camera, start pairing in its Bluetooth menu (the screen that waits for a new device). If the camera lists T1000-E from an earlier pairing, delete it there first: the camera does not pair a card it already knows.
  3. Click Pair. The card waits up to 2 minutes for GPS time, then looks for the camera for up to 1 minute, and tries up to 3 times.

Forget the paired camera? The card stops connecting to it until you pair them again. Before that, also delete T1000-E in the camera's Bluetooth menu.

    About this page

    The page runs only in your browser and sends nothing over the internet. It talks to the card over USB at 115200 baud and sends it only these commands: v (version), status (the card's state), pair and pair off (start and cancel pairing), unpair (forget the camera), clock on and clock off (whether the card sets the camera's clock), sound loud, sound quiet and sound off (how loud the card is), tz (show the time zone) and tz with the zone you chose. Offsets shown in the list come from your browser and are only a guide; the card uses its own copy of the tz database.
